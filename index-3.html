Help articles & FAQs

Search the most common questions
How does the DEX 25% stop-loss rule work?

TRADE SMARTER estimates a stop MCAP so that the token's percentage move would correspond to about 25% of the allocated capital, before fees and slippage. MCAP is not a guaranteed execution price; liquidity, token taxes and slippage can materially change the result.

How does the Futures 30% margin risk rule work?

The calculator targets a loss of 30% of the entered margin. It adjusts the stop-price distance using leverage. Funding, trading fees, maintenance margin, mark price and exchange-specific rules can change the actual outcome.

What does Break-Even (BE) do?

The BE control changes the planned stop-loss to the entry price. The suggested 2R level is a planning trigger only. TRADE SMARTER does not monitor the market or move a live exchange stop automatically in this prototype.

Why is my DEX target profit only an estimate?

Profit is estimated from the entry MCAP, target MCAP and allocated capital. Actual token fills depend on pool liquidity, price impact, token taxes, fees and slippage. A target MCAP is not an order submission.

Are prices live and are trades executed?

No. Market-watch values are illustrative. There is no live market feed, wallet connection, exchange API, or order execution in this standalone prototype.

Where is my trade journal stored?

Journal entries, settings and support requests are saved in this browser on this device in this version. They are not yet synced to your Supabase account. Export backups regularly and avoid clearing browser data before exporting.

Can I register a real account here?

Email registration and sign-in use Supabase authentication. Email verification may be required. Phone is profile information only; SMS OTP is not enabled. Journal entries, preferences and support requests remain in this browser in this version.
